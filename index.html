About Hitoyoshi

A Castle Town Frozen in Time

Often referred to as "Kyushu's Little Kyoto," Hitoyoshi is a historic castle town in the southern mountainous basin of Kumamoto Prefecture. Ruled by the Sagara clan for nearly 700 unbroken years — from the Kamakura period through the Meiji Restoration — the town preserves a nostalgic streetscape of samurai heritage, sacred thatched shrines, and natural beauty.

The mighty Kuma River, celebrated as one of Japan's three fastest-flowing rivers, carves straight through the city center, offering traditional boat cruises and thrilling river adventures. Along its scenic banks, more than 50 natural hot spring sources provide rejuvenating alkaline onsen baths, making Hitoyoshi a timeless destination for cultural travelers and outdoor lovers alike.

Historic Landmark

Hitoyoshi Castle Ruins 人吉城跡

Also known as Sengetsu-jō (Crescent Moon Castle), these monumental ruins overlook the turquoise Kuma River. Established in 1198 and ruled by the Sagara clan for 35 generations over 700 years, the stronghold features rare European-influenced curved stone ramparts (musha-gaeshi) engineered to repel invaders, offering panoramic vistas across the entire river valley.

Fumoto-machi, Hitoyoshi
1 – 2 hours
Free Admission

National Treasure of Japan

Aoi Aso Shrine 青井阿蘇神社

Designated an official National Treasure of Japan in 2008, Aoi Aso Shrine was founded in 806 CE. Its five interconnected structures feature steeply pitched thatched roofs (kayabuki) and vibrant Momoyama-style decorative wood carvings, embodying 1,200 years of deep spiritual devotion and architectural excellence.

Kamiaoi-machi, Hitoyoshi
45 – 60 mins
Free Admission

Scenic Waterway & Rapids

Kuma River & Boat Cruise 球磨川下り

Celebrated as one of Japan's three fastest-flowing rivers, the emerald Kuma River carves straight through the Hitoyoshi Basin. Experience Kumagawa Kudari, an iconic 700-year-old river expedition guided by skilled boatmen with long bamboo poles traversing dynamic rapids and limestone bluffs.

Hawatashi-machi, Hitoyoshi
1.5 – 2 hours

Therapeutic Mineral Baths

Hitoyoshi Onsen 人吉温泉

Renowned since the late 15th century, Hitoyoshi is blessed with over 50 natural hot spring sources along the river valley. The silky, weakly alkaline waters gently rejuvenate skin and soothe tired muscles. Authentic public bathhouses (sento) made of aromatic cedar offer timeless moments of quiet reflection.

Basin Onsen District
1 – 2 hours
¥300 – ¥800

Heritage Railway

SL Hitoyoshi Heritage Train 蒸気機関車 SL人吉

Powered by the legendary JR Kyushu locomotive No. 58654, Japan's oldest active commercial steam train. Built in 1922 during the Taisho period, this beloved locomotive traveled the scenic Hisatsu Line through mist-draped mountain tunnels and dramatic river gorges, welcoming travelers from across the globe.

JR Hitoyoshi Station

Natural Wonder

Kaname & Ryuomon Waterfalls 鹿目の滝

Selected among Japan's Top 100 Waterfalls, Kaname Falls thunders 36 meters down columnar basalt volcanic cliffs into a secluded, crystal-clear mountain pool. Divided into the thundering Odaki (male falls) and gentle Medaki (female falls), the lush cedar gorge offers crisp mountain air, mossy stones, and tranquil hiking trails.

Kaname-machi, Hitoyoshi
1 – 1.5 hours
Free Admission

Must-Do Experiences

Top 6 Recommended Activities in Hitoyoshi

An essential curated itinerary for travelers seeking an authentic blend of nature, heritage, and wellness.

  1. 01

    Kumagawa Kudari Wooden Boat Expedition

    Experience a 700-year-old river expedition guided by master boatmen with long bamboo poles through the emerald Kuma River rapids.

  2. 02

    Thermal Mineral Soaking at Hitoyoshi Onsen

    Rejuvenate in over 50 natural hot spring sources and historic cedarwood public bathhouses dating back to the samurai era.

  3. 03

    Exploring Aoi Aso Shrine & Crescent Castle

    Walk through 1,200 years of Shinto devotion at the thatched National Treasure shrine and the curved stone ramparts of Sengetsu-jō.

  4. 04

    Riding the Taisho-Era SL Hitoyoshi Steam Train

    Travel in vintage carriages powered by Japan's oldest operational steam train (No. 58654) through picturesque mountain ravines.

  5. 05

    Tasting 500-Year-Old Kuma Shōchū & River Ayu

    Savor authentic WTO-designated rice shōchū paired with freshly charcoal-grilled sweetfish harvested directly from pure river waters.

  6. 06

    Nature Trekking at Kaname & Ryuomon Waterfalls

    Hike the tranquil mossy trails leading to the 36-meter twin volcanic cascades of Kaname Falls, among Japan's Top 100 Waterfalls.

Getting There

Access & Location

Hitoyoshi is well-connected by rail and highway. The compact town center is easily walkable or navigated by rental bicycle.

By Train

JR Hisatsu Line from Kumamoto Station (~1.5 hours) or take the SL Hitoyoshi steam locomotive for a scenic journey on weekends.

By Car

Approximately 1.5 hours from Kumamoto City via the Kyushu Expressway. Free parking available at major attractions.

Nearest Airport

Kumamoto Aso Airport (KMJ) — about 1.5 hours by car or airport bus + train connection.
